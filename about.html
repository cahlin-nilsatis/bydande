About

Home design and structural engineering are not one practice — they're two, working as partners.

As partners, we strive for honesty and clarity. Our first job is to understand the client's vision and needs, not to present our own. We value timeliness, direct communication, and the occasional face-to-face meeting over a drink or a meal.

Over the years, we have collaborated with excellent clients on outstanding projects. However, it is still too early to comment on the work's success — a building exists in time, and its usefulness is directly related to how well it continues to perform.

We are proud of our Utah home design and consider ourselves experts in design and engineering for the typical homeowner, who must first suit the house or other building to the client's needs.
